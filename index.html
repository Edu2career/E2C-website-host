04 / A few useful answers

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still curious? Ask the founder.

Who is Edu2Career for?

We're building for students in educational institutions who want to explore career paths and prepare for the job market.

Can I use the platform today?

This website introduces Edu2Career and the product we plan to build. It doesn't offer accounts or live career guidance yet. Contact the founder for the current product stage and availability.

How would AI support the guidance?

We plan to use AI to interpret student profiles, explain possible career paths, identify skill gaps, and support learning and interview preparation. We intend to ground that guidance in information students provide and curated career resources.

Will Edu2Career choose a career for me?

No. You decide which careers to explore, what to learn, and which opportunities to pursue. The planned guidance supports those decisions; it doesn't guarantee a job.
